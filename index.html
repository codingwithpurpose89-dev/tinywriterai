Tiny Writer AI

Rephrase or explain text in any app (Slack, a pull request, an email) right where you selected it. No tab to switch to, no window to click out of.

Download for Mac

macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Windows version coming soon. Free while in beta.

How it works

  • Rephrase: select some text and press ⌘ ⇧ R. The rewritten version replaces your selection when you accept it.
  • Explain: select something you don't follow and press ⌘ ⇧ E. You get a short explanation you can copy, and your text stays untouched.

It keeps the language you wrote in, and it updates itself when a new version ships.
